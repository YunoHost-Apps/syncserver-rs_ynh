This version switches the internal of syncserver from MariaDB to PostgreSQL. The transition should be seamless.

However, no cleanup of old databases is done. If MariaDB remains installed after the upgrade (as a dependency of another app) please manually remove `syncserver_rs` and `syncserver_rs_tokenserver`  MariaDB databases (i.e. with [phpMyAdmin](https://apps.yunohost.org/app/phpmyadmin)).
